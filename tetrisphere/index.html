How to play

  • 🌀 The sphere is wrapped in layers of tetromino pieces.
  • 🎯 Move the cursor over the sphere. Green means your piece will blast — red means it won't.
  • 💥 Drop your piece onto 2 or more touching pieces of the same shape (side-by-side, diagonal, or stacked) to blast the whole group off.
  • 💔 A drop that blasts nothing costs a heart. Lose all 3 and it's game over.
  • ✋ Slide: grab a piece, reposition it, set it down to line up bigger blasts.
  • ⏱ Blasting adds time. Clear every layer to expose the core and clear the stage.
  • 🔥 Consecutive blasts build your combo multiplier.
